# Menu calendar

The menu can show a month or a week above its meetings, so the user can look
at any day without leaving the menu bar.

- The calendar is chosen under **Menu bar** in the General settings: a month, a
  week, or none. It is off until the user turns it on, and the choice is shared
  with the user's other Macs like the rest of the settings.
- The menu opens on today every time. Choosing a day lists all of that day's
  meetings, those that have ended included, in the place of today's and
  tomorrow's.
- The month and the week page the same way: back, forward, and back to today.
  The meetings listed always belong to a day on screen.
- Each day shows a dot in the colour of every account with a meeting that day.
- Today's and tomorrow's meetings are the ones the regular refresh reads. The
  other days are read when the calendar shows them, and only then; until they
  are read the menu says it is loading them, and when they cannot be read it
  says so instead of showing the day as empty.
- Meetings read for the calendar never cut in.
- With no calendar, the menu lists today's and tomorrow's meetings as described
  in [accounts and sync](accounts-and-sync.md).
