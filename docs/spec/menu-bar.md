# Menu bar

The next meeting label can be shown, hidden, or switched by right-clicking
the menu bar icon. Hiding the label keeps the icon available to open the menu.

Private mode replaces the meeting name in the label while keeping its countdown.
It can stay enabled, stay disabled, or switch on right-click. Visibility takes
priority when right-click switches the label itself; privacy controls are
unavailable while the label is hidden.

Preferences are shared between Macs. Right-click switches last until the app
quits and do not change the saved preferences.
