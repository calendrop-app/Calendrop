# Language

Calendrop's own words — the menu, the cut-in, the settings, its error messages
and the page the browser shows after sign-in — are in English or Japanese.

- Calendrop follows the order of languages in macOS and shows the first of them
  it has. When it has none of them, it shows English.
- A language can be given to Calendrop alone, either under **Language** in its
  General settings or for Calendrop in macOS's Language & Region settings; each
  shows what the other chose. The choice belongs to the Mac, not to the settings
  shared through iCloud Drive.
- The language is chosen at launch. A new choice takes effect when Calendrop
  restarts, which the settings offer to do; until then the interface stays in
  one language.
- Dates written out in words, such as the days in the menu, follow the same
  language.
- What comes from the calendars — meeting titles, calendar names, people's
  names, descriptions — is shown as written.
